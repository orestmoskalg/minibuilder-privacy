Mini Builder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · App: Mini Builder (package mini.builder)

This policy explains how the Mini Builder app for Android (“the app”) handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data. It has no accounts or sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. The current version does not access the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your builds, saved levels, game settings and material stock are stored only on your own device, in the app’s local storage. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibration permission, for short haptic feedback during play. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or any other personal data.

Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no third-party ads and processes no payments. If rewarded ads (Google AdMob) or in-app purchases (Google Play Billing) are added in a future version, those Google services may process device identifiers and purchase information under Google’s own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). This policy will be updated before such a version is released.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
